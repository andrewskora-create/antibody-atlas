Screen an antibody for developability liabilities

Paste a variable domain. Review potential chemical liabilities, charge and hydrophobicity, then explore candidate substitutions with their predicted tradeoffs. Everything runs on this computer.

Built on community research. ANARCII/ANARCI · ImmuneBuilder · FreeSASA · Paragraph · AntiBERTy · OpenMM/PDBFixer · ThermoMPNN · NGL · OGRDB · Thera-SAbDab · UniProt. DeepSP and PROPERMAB are optional. Atlas integrates these tools and adds its own screening rules. Meet the methods, authors and original projects →

Sequences stay on this machine. Nothing is uploaded. Structure prediction adds about a minute and unlocks the exposure and paratope columns. Three models takes about three times as long and reports the worst-case exposure across them, plus where they disagree — worth it when a site is about to decide an experiment. Supplying your own structure replaces the prediction entirely, and geometric results are calculated from the supplied coordinates rather than a newly predicted backbone. If the file contains the antigen, antibody–antigen distances can be calculated directly from that complex.

Screen a panelseveral molecules at once, ranked side by side

Paste a multi-FASTA panel. Pair the chains by name: >MAB01|H and >MAB01|L. Protein or DNA both work. This runs the sequence-only tier in the background, one molecule at a time. For large panels or for structure, use the command-line runner: python run_batch.py panel.fasta --out results/.

Click a molecule name to open it in the full screen, or tick rows and download their reports.

    What this does and does not tell you

    Every priority here is a screening heuristic for ordering experiments, not a prediction of behaviour. A sequence motif is a pattern, not an observed modification: confirm deamidation and isomerization by peptide mapping and oxidation by forced degradation before engineering anything away. Substitutions are ranked for conservativeness, positional precedent and surface consequence — none of that predicts retained binding, potency, expression or thermostability, and every candidate needs binding and activity testing. Paratope prediction does not know this antibody's antigen; a position predicted not to contact can still matter to affinity.
